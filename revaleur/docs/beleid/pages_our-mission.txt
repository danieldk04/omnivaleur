OUR MISSION
 Revaleur started in August 2024 to make designer vintage clothing trustworthy, transparent, and sustainable. Authentic luxury resale of verified vintage.
 WHERE WE COME FROM 
 We launched Revaleur in August 2024 to fix what most vintage shops overlook: unclear sizing, hidden flaws, and a lack of transparency.
 What started as a personal frustration became a mission — to make buying vintage feel as reliable and effortless as buying new.
 Every item is hand‑measured, condition‑graded, and selected with care. We photograph each piece honestly, describe it clearly, and ship it worldwide with attention to detail.
 But we’re not stopping there. Revaleur is evolving into more than a webshop. A brand extension that could grow into a showroom, capsule line, or content studio. 
 Always rooted in clarity, trust, and timeless style.
 Because secondhand shouldn’t mean second‑rate. It should mean smarter choices, meaningful impact, and fashion that lasts.
