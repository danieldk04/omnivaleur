Shipping policy
 Processing and Handling
 Orders ship Monday–Saturday, usually within 1 business day of placing your order.
 All orders include tracking. You will receive a tracking link via email once your order has been shipped.
 Delivery Time & Risk of Loss
 Please note that delivery times are estimates and start from the day your order is shipped, not the day it is placed.
 Risk of Loss: Under Dutch and European law, we bear the risk of loss or damage to the product during transport until you (or a third party designated by you, other than the carrier) have physically received the goods.
 Unless a longer delivery period is explicitly agreed upon, all orders will be delivered within a maximum of 30 days from the date of purchase.

 International Shipping (Outside the EU)
 Customs, Duties, and Import Fees: 
 Orders shipped to countries outside the European Union (EU) may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees once the shipment reaches your country.
 These charges are not included in the item price or shipping costs.
 These fees are the buyer's responsibility and are typically collected by the shipping carrier upon delivery.
 We advise you to check with your country's customs office to determine what these additional costs will be prior to making your purchase.
