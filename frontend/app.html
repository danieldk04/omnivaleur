Refresh

Bump old listings that sank in the "Newest" sort, on Vinted, Marktplaats and 2dehands. Rate-limited on purpose: on Vinted 1x per listing per 14 days and 8 refreshes/day; on Marktplaats and 2dehands 1x per 21 days and only 3/day, because Marktplaats charges for bumping a listing and therefore watches remove-and-repost far more closely than Vinted does. Nothing here fakes engagement, and nothing is guaranteed to change ranking. eBay relist-ended lives under Items for now — more platforms will get their own tab here as refresh support expands.

Every marketplace sets its own rules on relisting and automation, and those rules can change. Omnivaleur is not liable for any warning, suspension, ban, lost listing, or fee resulting from a refresh or relist. Use it in line with each platform's terms, and keep your refresh volume reasonable.
ℹ️ What actually happens when you refresh (and what stays open)

The "bump" comes from a fresh listing date. Vinted & Marktplaats sort "Newest" by when a listing was created. A relist deletes the old listing now and recreates it after a randomised delay (45–240 min), so it gets a brand-new date and climbs back to the top — while looking like normal, organic activity instead of a scripted burst.

The recreate is a faithful copy. Right before deleting, we snapshot the full live listing (all photos, description, brand, size, condition, colour, material, category) straight from the platform, so the new listing matches the old one.

Photos are re-encoded, not re-used as-is. Each photo is lightly re-processed (tiny crop + imperceptible tone shift + re-encode) so the platform sees genuinely new images, not a duplicate upload — with no visible quality change.

What each platform does
  • Vinted, Marktplaats & 2dehands — delete + delayed recreate, reusing the same automation that already publishes your listings. Vinted also supports a lower-risk content-only nudge: it re-orders your photos (never the first/main one) and re-saves the listing, without deleting or recreating anything.
  • eBay — relist ended only. eBay's duplicate-listing policy forbids bumping a live listing, so this only republishes listings that have already ended (sold/expired/withdrawn) via eBay's own relist endpoint.
🖥️
Keep Chrome running until the recreate is done
The extension does the delete and the delayed recreate. Chrome must stay open (a background window is fine — it doesn't need focus) so the scheduled recreate can fire. You do not need to keep this dashboard tab open, and you don't need to keep the Vinted/Marktplaats tab open. If Chrome is fully closed during the wait, the recreate simply runs the next time you open Chrome.

📋 How it works — step by step

1
Install the Chrome extension
Download the Omnivaleur extension from the Chrome Web Store and log in with your account credentials. The extension runs silently in the background — you don't need to open it again after setup.
2
Create an item
Click + New item, fill in the title, description, price, size and upload your photos. Write everything in English — Omnivaleur handles the Dutch translation for Marktplaats & 2dehands automatically.
3
Choose platforms and publish
Select which marketplaces to list on — Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted or Shopify — and click Save & publish. The publishing job is instantly added to the queue.
4
Extension publishes automatically
Within 15 seconds the Chrome extension picks up the job, opens the marketplace in a background tab, fills in all fields, uploads your photos and submits the listing. Fully automated — the tab closes when done.
5
Item sold? Mark it once
When your item sells on any platform, click Mark as sold in the dashboard. Omnivaleur automatically removes the listing from all other platforms — no double sales.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Why is my listing stuck in "Queued"?
The Chrome extension is either not installed, not logged in, or Chrome isn't running. Click the extension icon and make sure it shows "Extension active — ready to publish". Once active, it picks up the job within 15 seconds.
The extension opens a tab but the listing doesn't get submitted — what's wrong?
You are probably not logged in to that marketplace in Chrome. Open Marktplaats, 2dehands or Vinted manually, log in, then try publishing again. The extension uses your existing browser session.
Do I need to log in to the marketplaces separately?
Yes — once. Log in to Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted in Chrome at least once. Your session will be remembered. Omnivaleur never stores your marketplace passwords.
Can I write my listings in English?
Yes — always write in English. Omnivaleur automatically translates your title and description to Dutch for Marktplaats and 2dehands. Vinted and Shopify use the English text directly.
What happens when an item sells?
Click Mark as sold on the item. Omnivaleur queues delist jobs for all other platforms and the extension removes those listings automatically — preventing double sales.
Which platforms are supported?
Currently: Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted, eBay and Shopify. Etsy, Kleinanzeigen and Facebook Marketplace are in development. Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted use the Chrome extension; eBay and Shopify connect via their official API.
Are my marketplace passwords stored?
No. Omnivaleur never sees or stores your marketplace passwords. The extension uses the browser session you already have open. Only your Omnivaleur account password is used to log in to this dashboard.
Can I use Omnivaleur on multiple devices?
The dashboard works on any device and browser. The Chrome extension needs to be installed on the specific computer where publishing should happen. Jobs are processed by whichever Chrome has the extension active.
