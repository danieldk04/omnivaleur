1. Who we are

Omnivaleur is a product of Revaleur, a company registered in the Netherlands (KVK 86792423). We provide a Chrome extension and web application that allows users to cross-list second-hand items on European marketplaces including Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted, Shopify and WooCommerce.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at info@omnivaleur.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: email address and password (hashed) when you create an account via Supabase Auth.
  • Billing data: payment method details are processed directly by Stripe. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Listing data: item titles, descriptions, photos, prices and other product information you enter to crosslist. Photos are stored in our own storage bucket at Cloudflare (R2) and served from img.omnivaleur.com.
  • Automated text help: when Omnivaleur suggests a category, translates a title or description, or writes a listing text from your photos, the text (and, for the photo feature, the photos) of that one item is sent to an AI provider (see section 5). We do not send your email address or account details.
  • Emails we send you: your email address, the messages we send (such as trial reminders and product updates) and whether they were delivered. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time.
  • Free tools (omnivaleur.com/tools): the tools work without an account. The numbers and text you type into a tool are calculated in your browser and are not sent to us. They also appear in the page link so you can share it; anyone who has that link sees them. We only receive an email address if you give it to us, in two cases. If you ask for a spreadsheet, we ask for your address and a consent box for occasional tips about selling second-hand, and we email you a download link. We only store your address once you open that link, which also confirms the address is yours. If you ask a tool to email you your result, we send you that one email and, only if you tick the box for tips, we also store your address. When we store an address we keep the address, your language, which tool you used, the time and your consent. To prevent abuse we keep a short-lived count of requests per IP address in server memory, which is not stored permanently.
  • WooCommerce connection: the REST API key your shop gives Omnivaleur when you approve the connection (or that you paste yourself) is stored on our servers. It is used only to read your products and orders and to update products and stock in your shop. You can revoke it at any time in WooCommerce under Settings, Advanced, REST API.
  • Platform session data: session cookies for connected marketplaces (Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify) are stored locally in your Chrome extension and are never transmitted to our servers in readable form.
  • Usage data: server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) for security and debugging.

3. Why we process your data (legal basis)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to provide the Omnivaleur service — creating your account, processing crosslisting jobs, managing your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): security monitoring, fraud prevention, and service improvement, including measuring how our website and app are used with Google Analytics.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): sending you tips and updates about selling second-hand when you tick the box for it while using our free tools. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and the email we send you will say how. The single result email you ask for is sent at your request.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): to comply with Dutch and EU legal requirements, including invoice retention for tax purposes.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Account data: retained for as long as your account is active. When you use Delete account in the dashboard, your login, items, listings, photos, connections and subscription are deleted immediately and your subscription is stopped. If you ask us by email instead, we do it within 30 days.
  • Billing / invoice records: 7 years (Dutch fiscal retention requirement). These stay with Stripe and in our administration after you delete your account.
  • Free-tool subscribers (tips): until you withdraw your consent. After that we keep only your address on the suppression list described below, so we do not email you again. If you only asked for a result email and did not tick the box, we do not add your address to any list; our email provider keeps a delivery log like it does for all our emails.
  • If you unsubscribed from our emails, we keep your email address on a suppression list so we do not email you again. This is the only thing we keep beyond the above.
  • Server logs: maximum 90 days.
  • Photos on third-party platforms and the ads you published there are not deleted by us. They sit in your own account at those platforms.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following processors, all of whom have agreed to EU-compliant data processing agreements:

  • Supabase Inc. (database and authentication) — servers in the EU (AWS eu-west-1).
  • Stripe Inc. (payment processing) — Stripe is certified PCI-DSS Level 1 and compliant with EU data transfer rules.
  • Railway Corp. (hosting) — our backend server runs on Railway infrastructure in the Netherlands (Amsterdam, EU West). Your data is stored in the EU at Supabase and is processed on this EU server whenever you use the app.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (photo storage and delivery, DNS and security) — your listing photos are stored in Cloudflare R2.
  • Resend and Zoho (email delivery and our mailbox) — your email address and the emails we exchange with you.
  • Google LLC (Gemini) and Anthropic PBC (Claude) — AI providers used for category suggestions, translations and listing texts, as described in section 2. Only the content of the item involved is sent.
  • Google LLC (Google Analytics) — statistics about how our website and app are used, see section 8.

When you list items on third-party platforms (Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, WooCommerce), your listing content is transmitted to those platforms under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. International data transfers

Supabase stores your account and listing data in the EU. Our backend server at Railway is located in the Netherlands. Some of our other processors (for example Stripe, Cloudflare, Google, Anthropic, Resend and Zoho) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, this happens under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or an equivalent safeguard, ensuring an equivalent level of protection to that provided under GDPR.

7. Your rights under GDPR

As a data subject within the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15).
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16).
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17).
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances (Art. 18).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20).
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)), for example for tips you signed up for through our free tools. Email info@omnivaleur.com and we stop.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

You can use most of these rights yourself in the dashboard, under Account, Privacy & your data: download everything we hold about you (access and portability) or delete your account (erasure). For anything else, or if you prefer, email info@omnivaleur.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

The Omnivaleur web app uses functional cookies and browser storage required to maintain your login session. Our website and web app also load Google Analytics (GA4), which sets analytics cookies to measure how pages are used (pages visited, device and browser type, approximate region). We use it only for our own statistics, not for advertising. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. You can block it with your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or ask us to remove your data at info@omnivaleur.com. We do not use advertising cookies. The Chrome extension stores marketplace session data in Chrome's local extension storage — this never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

9. Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures: HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, hashed passwords (bcrypt via Supabase Auth), and access controls limiting who within Revaleur can access production data. We perform regular security reviews.

10. Children

Omnivaleur is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email for material changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
