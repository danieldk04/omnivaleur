Built-in protections
Omnivaleur works smart in the background
Every platform has its own rules and restrictions. Omnivaleur knows them all and resolves them automatically — so you never have to worry.
🔄
Marktplaats auto-relist
✓ Active — every 6 hours
Free Marktplaats listings expire silently after 28 days. Omnivaleur checks every 6 hours and automatically relists after 27 days so you stay visible.
Without this: listing disappears after 28 days without warning.
🛡️
Vinted photo protection
✓ Active — every upload
Vinted detects identical photos from other platforms via pixel-hashing and may ban your account. Omnivaleur slightly modifies each photo via canvas rendering — invisible to the eye, but a unique hash for Vinted.
Without this: risk of account ban on Vinted.
🧹
2dehands formatting fix
✓ Active — every publish
2dehands only supports 7 HTML tags in descriptions. Other tags crash the editor. Omnivaleur automatically strips all unsupported formatting before publishing.
Without this: publish fails or description is empty.
🌐
Auto-translation
✓ Active — Marktplaats & 2dehands
Marktplaats and 2dehands are Dutch-language platforms. You enter your listings in English — Omnivaleur automatically translates titles and descriptions to Dutch before publishing.
Without this: English text visible on Dutch-language platforms.
⚡
Sale sync
✓ Active — all platforms
Once an item sells on one platform, all other active listings are automatically removed. No double sales, no manual cleanup.
Without this: risk of double sales and negative reviews.
📋
Marktplaats editor fix
✓ Active — every publish
Marktplaats uses its own rich-text editor (Lexical) that does not respond to standard DOM input. Omnivaleur fills the editor correctly via the internal EditorState API.
Without this: description stays empty on publish.
🖼️
Photo mirroring on import
✓ Active — every import
Marketplace photo links are signed and expire, and one platform's CDN refuses to serve another's page. Omnivaleur copies every imported photo into its own storage first, so it stays publishable forever.
Without this: imported items publish without a single image.
🐢
Rate-limited refreshing
✓ Active — per listing & per day
Refresh and relist actions are capped per listing and per day, with randomised delays between steps. Every strategy uses the platform's own mechanism — eBay's relist flow, Etsy's renewal, a plain delete-and-recreate elsewhere.
Without this: bulk bumping looks like scripted spam.
