Every screen you get, exactly as it works today

These are screenshots of the real Omnivaleur dashboard. Nothing here is a roadmap promise.

Dashboard

Your stock and revenue at a glance, plus the live status of the browser extension that does the publishing for Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted.

If the extension is asleep, you see it here before a job silently fails.

Items

One inventory, split by lifecycle instead of one endless list. Select rows and publish, reprice, delist or delete them in bulk.

Per-platform prices are stored per item, so the same coat can be €189 on Marktplaats and €199 on eBay.

Analytics

Revenue, profit and margin over 7, 30, 90 or 365 days or all time, built from your real sale prices and purchase costs, not from list prices.

Rows where the real sale price or purchase cost is still missing are flagged, so a number is never quietly guessed for you.

Messages & bids

Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted have no seller API for messages, so the extension reads the unread badge from your own logged-in session and shows all of it in one place.

Counts only. Omnivaleur never stores or reads message contents: you get a number and a deep link, and you reply on the platform itself.

Import listings

Already selling? Scan your existing Vinted, Marktplaats and 2dehands listings and pull them in. Brand, size, colour, condition and photos are parsed out, and each item is sorted into the right category automatically.

Imported photos are mirrored into Omnivaleur’s own storage, so they still publish to the next platform months later. Categorisation that isn’t confident is flagged for you instead of guessed.

Refresh and auto-relist

Old listings sink in “newest first” sorting. Marktplaats and 2dehands relist automatically after 27 days, eBay republishes ended offers through its own relist flow. On Vinted refresh is off by default: you decide to switch it on, at your own risk.

Deliberately rate-limited per listing and per day, with random delays between steps. Nothing here fakes engagement.

Stale stock

Items that have been live for ages without selling, with a suggested price cut that gets deeper the longer they sit. Reprice a whole selection at once.

The ladder is 5% from 30 days, 10% from 60, 15% from 90 and 20% from 180, rounded to …,99, and it never suggests going below what you paid.

Margin calculator

Enter your purchase price and the profit you want. You get the minimum selling price per platform, fees included, before you list, not after you’ve been paid.

The same €85 in your pocket needs a different sticker price on every marketplace.

Platforms

Connect once. eBay, Shopify and WooCommerce use their official APIs; Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted and Facebook run through the free Omnivaleur Chrome extension on your own logged-in session.

About that beta: Facebook doesn’t officially allow automated listing and actively looks for it. There is a real chance Facebook flags or blocks your account, so use it deliberately, ideally on a separate account. The app tells you the same before you switch it on.

Omnivaleur works smart in the background

Every platform has its own rules and restrictions. Omnivaleur knows them and resolves them automatically.

Marktplaats auto-relist

Every 6 hours

Free Marktplaats listings expire silently after 28 days. Omnivaleur checks every 6 hours and automatically relists after 27 days so you stay visible.

Without this: listing disappears after 28 days without warning.

Vinted photo protection

Every upload

Vinted detects identical photos from other platforms via pixel-hashing and may ban your account. Omnivaleur slightly modifies each photo, invisible to the eye.

Without this: risk of account ban on Vinted.

2dehands formatting fix

Every publish

2dehands only supports 7 HTML tags in descriptions. Other tags crash the editor. Omnivaleur automatically strips all unsupported formatting before publishing.

Without this: publish fails or description is empty.

Auto-translation

Marktplaats, 2dehands

Marktplaats and 2dehands are Dutch-language platforms. Omnivaleur automatically translates titles and descriptions to Dutch before publishing.

Without this: English text visible on Dutch-language platforms.

Sale sync

All platforms

Once an item sells on one platform, all other active listings are automatically removed. No double sales, no manual cleanup.

Without this: risk of double sales and negative reviews.

Marktplaats editor fix

Every publish

Marktplaats uses its own text editor, which does not respond to normal input. Omnivaleur fills it the way the editor itself expects.

Without this: description stays empty on publish.

Photo mirroring on import

Every import

Marketplace photo links are signed and expire. Omnivaleur copies every imported photo into its own storage first, so it stays publishable.

Without this: imported items publish without a single image.

Rate-limited refreshing

Per listing and per day

Refresh and relist actions are capped per listing and per day, with randomised delays between steps. Every strategy uses the platform’s own mechanism.

Without this: bulk bumping looks like scripted spam.
