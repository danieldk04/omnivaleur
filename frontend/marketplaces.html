From one item to all of Europe

Omnivaleur makes cross-listing so easy you'll never have to think about it again.

  1. Install the Chrome extension

    The extension runs in the background and does the placing on Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted through your own logged-in accounts. Install once, always active.

  2. Import your existing items

    Scan your existing listings on Marktplaats, 2dehands or Vinted and import them in bulk. Photos, description, price and category land in your dashboard.

  3. Choose your marketplaces

    Select per item which marketplaces it goes to.

  4. Omnivaleur publishes everything automatically

    Our extension fills in forms, adapts categories per platform and posts your item. You don't have to do a thing.

  5. Sell, sync, repeat

    Once an item sells on one platform, Omnivaleur automatically removes it everywhere else. No more double sales or outdated listings.

Everything about the marketplaces

Which marketplaces are available now?
Currently Marktplaats (NL), Vinted (pan-EU), 2dehands (BE), eBay Europe, Shopify and WooCommerce are fully integrated. Facebook Marketplace works as a beta: Facebook doesn't officially allow automated listing, so use it deliberately. Etsy, Kleinanzeigen (DE) and Wallapop (ES) are coming soon. We continuously add new platforms based on user feedback.
Do I need an account on every marketplace?
Yes, you need your own account on each marketplace you want to publish to. Omnivaleur posts items on your behalf via your own logged-in browser, so everything stays under your name and reputation.
Does Omnivaleur work in all EU countries?
Yes. Omnivaleur is designed for all 27 EU member states. Platform availability per country varies (Marktplaats is only active in the Netherlands, for example) but the dashboard works everywhere. We continuously expand platform coverage per country.
What happens when I sell an item on one platform?
Once you mark a sale in Omnivaleur (or the extension detects it), all other listings are automatically removed or deactivated. This prevents double sales and outdated listings on other platforms.
Are descriptions automatically translated?
Yes. You write it once. Marktplaats, 2dehands and eBay get Dutch and Shopify gets English, translated on every placement. Vinted and WooCommerce get exactly the text you wrote.
Are all marketplaces included in every plan?
All live platforms are available in every paid plan. There are no extra costs per platform. You pay one price and get access to everything your plan offers.
How is Omnivaleur different from Vendoo or List Perfectly?
Vendoo and List Perfectly are primarily focused on the US market (Poshmark, Mercari, eBay US). Omnivaleur focuses exclusively on European platforms: Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted and eBay, with Kleinanzeigen and Wallapop planned. We also handle what Europe needs, such as listings in the right language and the correct platform categories, and we offer a free DAC7 checker.
Can I use multiple accounts for the same marketplace?
Currently Omnivaleur supports one account per marketplace. Multiple accounts per platform is on the roadmap for professional resellers and teams. Contact us if this is urgent for you.
Which Chrome extension do I need?
Omnivaleur works via an official Chrome extension that you install once. The extension communicates with your Omnivaleur dashboard and automatically posts items to the marketplaces. Without the extension, automatic publishing to Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted and Facebook does not work, but you can still import and manage your inventory in the dashboard.
Is my data safe and GDPR-compliant?
Yes. Your account and listing data are stored in the EU and we do not sell your data. We only share it with the processors named in our privacy policy. Your marketplace login credentials never leave your own browser: the extension always works via your own logged-in session.
