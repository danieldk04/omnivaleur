Etsy connection

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Status: we have applied for access to the Etsy API. The connection is not available yet and we give no date.

What the connection does

When a seller connects their own Etsy shop through Etsy’s own sign-in screen (OAuth), Omnivaleur does exactly four things:

  • Creates a listing in that seller’s own shop, only for items that qualify for Etsy (vintage, 20 years or older).
  • Reads the seller’s own listings, so no duplicates are created.
  • Removes or deactivates a listing as soon as the same item has sold on another channel.
  • Reads the seller’s own orders, to recognise a sale on Etsy and remove the item elsewhere.

What we do not do

  • No scraping of Etsy and no browser extension on Etsy. Only the official API.
  • No copying of Etsy listings to other marketplaces.
  • No sending of buyers to other sites. Etsy remains the sales and payment platform.
  • No use of Etsy data for AI training, analytics, advertising or sale to third parties.
  • No asking for or storing sellers’ passwords. Signing in happens at Etsy itself.

Data

We request only the permissions the four points above need and do not show or keep Etsy data longer than necessary. A seller can withdraw the connection at any time in their Etsy account. See also our privacy policy and terms.
