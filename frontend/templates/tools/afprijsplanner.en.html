Frequently asked questions

After how many days should I drop the price of an item?

Under 30 days you do not need to do anything. From 30 days online the price drops 5%, from 60 days 10%, from 90 days 15% and from 180 days 20%.

Can I sell below my purchase price?

The planner never suggests it. If the new price would be under what you paid, it says so and leaves the price alone.

Why do prices end in .99?

Such a price is usual on Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted. The planner therefore rounds down to an amount ending in .99, instead of an arbitrary amount like €41.37.

Is relisting better than dropping the price?

They do different things. Relisting puts your listing back on top, a lower price makes it more attractive. For an item that has been up for a long time a combination usually works better than either alone.
