The limit per marketplace

  • Marktplaats and 2dehands: 60 characters.
  • eBay: 80 characters.
  • Facebook Marketplace: 100 characters.

Spaces count. These are the limits Omnivaleur lists with: the listing forms of Marktplaats, 2dehands and Facebook, and eBay's title limit. Vinted is not included because we have not measured its limit.

The shortened version above works the way Omnivaleur does it: whole parts after a " - " go first, and if that is not enough it cuts at a whole word. That way no half sentence is left behind.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters can a Marktplaats title have?

At most 60 characters, spaces included. 2dehands uses the same limit.

How many characters can an eBay title have?

At most 80 characters. That is more room than on Marktplaats, so a title written for eBay often does not fit on Marktplaats.

What happens to a title that is too long?

The listing form cuts it off, often mid-word. Writing it shorter and putting the key words first works better than letting the form cut it.

Do emoji and spaces count?

Spaces do. An emoji counts as one character here, but a marketplace may count it differently. Leave emoji out when space is tight.
