Amounts exclude VAT. A business seller can usually reclaim the VAT on the fees. Not included: shipping you pay yourself, paid extras such as bumping or a top ad, and any tax on your profit.

What each marketplace charges the seller

Marktplaats

In most categories Marktplaats charges no commission, and the buyer pays for buyer protection. Paid extras, such as a top ad, and the business plan Marktplaats Pro are not included in the calculation.

2dehands

According to the 2dehands help centre, selling is free in most cases, with no commission or transaction fees. In Cars and Motorbikes you pay from the second listing. For a payment request with Bancontact, 2dehands charges a €0.40 service fee; the buyer pays it by default, and you can take it over if you want to.

Vinted

Sellers pay nothing on Vinted. The full amount goes into your Vinted wallet. The buyer pays the Vinted fee, which used to be called the buyer protection fee. Only optional services, such as bumping your item, cost money.

eBay.nl

  • Private seller: 11% of the total up to €1,990 and 2% above that, plus €0.35 per order (€0.05 under €10) and a 0.42% regulatory fee.
  • Business seller: usually 11% up to €990 per item and 2% above, plus €0.45 per order over €10 (€0.35 up to and including €10) and a 0.35% regulatory fee. This differs per category, for example 11% up to €400 for jewellery and watches and 6.5% up to €300 for photo and camera.
  • Listing fees (from €0.35 per listing for business sellers without a shop) are not included, nor is VAT on the fees.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to sell on Marktplaats?

In most categories Marktplaats charges no selling commission, and buyer protection is paid by the buyer. Paid extras such as a top ad or bumping cost money.

Is selling on Vinted free?

Yes, for the seller. You get the full amount in your Vinted wallet and the buyer pays the Vinted fee. Only optional services, such as bumping, cost money.

How much commission does eBay.nl charge?

Private sellers pay 11% up to €1,990 and 2% above. Business sellers usually pay 11% up to €990 and 2% above. A fixed amount per order and a regulatory fee come on top. See above for the amounts.

Why do I need to ask a higher price on eBay than on Vinted?

Because eBay takes commission on the sale amount, while Marktplaats, 2dehands and Vinted charge the seller nothing. For the same profit the eBay price has to be higher. The calculator shows how much higher per marketplace.

What does a listing on Marktplaats cost?

Marktplaats charges no commission in most categories. Paid extras, such as a top listing, do cost money and are not included in this calculation. When you post, you see what applies in your category.

Who pays for buyer protection on Marktplaats?

The buyer. For the seller there is no commission in most categories, so you can work out the asking price as purchase price plus profit.

What does a payment request on Marktplaats cost?

EUR 0.40 service fee per payment. The buyer pays it by default, on top of the agreed amount. You can also have it taken from the agreed amount so the buyer pays nothing extra. Tick the box in the calculator for that.

How fast do I get paid with buyer protection on Marktplaats?

If the buyer confirms everything is fine, the money is released. If the buyer does nothing, Marktplaats says you are paid automatically after 48 hours. If the buyer reports a problem, the payment partner holds the money while it investigates.
