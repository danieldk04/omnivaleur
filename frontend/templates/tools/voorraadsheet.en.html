Frequently asked questions

Is the spreadsheet really free?

Yes. You pay nothing, your email address is the trade. You get the spreadsheet by email, so we know the address is correct. You do not need an Omnivaleur account.

What does the DAC7 check in the yearly overview do?

Per platform it counts your sales and revenue in the chosen year. At 30 sales or more, or revenue above €2,000, it says the platform reports your details to the tax authority.

Does it work in Google Sheets and Numbers?

It is made and tested for Excel. It may work in other programs, but we have not tested that. Check the formulas there.

How many items fit?

500 rows. If you have more, copy the last row downwards.
